# EXP-004: isolated V1 weapon-search radius

[CODEX_ENHANCEMENT_2026-11-21]

Base: `8a87e8a467dde525d25fcf9cb5181087c6e55fd7` (official 2.10).
Branch: `experiment/v1-weapon-reach-2026-11-21`.

The only tactical change is the maximum pursuit distance for visible weapon
pickups, from 65 to 130 meters. Existing same-kind, full inventory, next-zone,
near-enemy, vertical-reach and urgent-zone filters apply. Navigation, firing,
energy management, cube/drop acquisition and pickup execution are V1 code.
No V2 changes or EXP-003 retaliation logic are included.

Hypothesis: missing nearby weapon opportunities can leave the mech gun-only;
wider safe pickup pursuit may improve subsequent combat. Risk: longer detours
may expose the mech to hunters or cost positioning time. This is a parameter
experiment, not an established improvement.

Local validation: 16 tests passed; official checker 300/300 in time, zero late,
stale, wrong-tick or bad messages; p50 3.6 ms, p99 9.5 ms, maximum 11.0 ms.
The official recording produces **zero action or goal changes** for this parameter,
so it cannot establish a tactical benefit. Six synthetic regressions verify
pursuit at 100 m, the 130 m cap, contested/roof/outside-zone rejection, urgent-zone
priority and duplicate/full-inventory rejection. They are not match simulations.
