## One hypothesis

V1 forbids every weapon from firing beyond 55 m in conservative mode. Under
continued incoming fire this leaves its gun silent even though damage already
prevents regeneration. The candidate allows gun retaliation at the current visible
target if that exact ID inflicted observed non-zone weapon damage within 0.6 s.
Six decision intervals bridge ordinary gaps between incoming gun shots without
maintaining a long-lived combat state. This interval is a test parameter, not an
officially optimized constant.

All aim, cooldown, ammunition, reload, range, line-of-fire and mortar-priority
gates are retained. Other weapon triggers, target priority, movement, boosts,
loot, upgrades and modes are unchanged. After the short window expires, quiet
retreat resumes its original firing restriction. Attacker memory expires and
uses only protocol-provided events.
