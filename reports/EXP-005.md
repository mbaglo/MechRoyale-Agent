weight 1.0. At a held goal and with one nearby enemy, moving directly away by
eight meters receives only 6.4 separation reward but costs eight goal meters.
The bot can therefore stay stationary despite the nearby opponent.

conservative weight remains 2.5 and the aggressive weight remains 0.8. The existing
75 m balanced desired range, collision veto, zone and projectile penalties,
movement candidates and energy policy remain intact. EXP-003's evaluated gun
retaliation is inherited; the unmeasured weapon-search experiment is not.

quiet and distant-threat stationary behavior, collision veto, and unchanged
aggressive weighting. All existing tests are retained. The official recording
does not exercise this change: the A/B audit gives zero movement differences,
and verifies every non-movement field matches the 2.19 source exactly.
