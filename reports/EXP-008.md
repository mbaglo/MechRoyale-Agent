## Hypothesis and implementation

Ground retreat can remain in the cooperating squad's line of fire, preventing the
five-second quiet interval required for health regeneration. A reachable roof
may interrupt pursuit and permit healing. This is a hypothesis; no match evidence
yet establishes that the house bots cannot reach or shoot the selected roof.

`RoofRefuge` caches up to nine interior points per building on the public static
map. Its 277 supported cached points are on roofs 10–36 m above local ground.
The active mech must have at least 85 energy, be grounded, not be under urgent
zone pressure, and see multiple nearby opponents or be injured near an opponent.
Selection requires a roof rise of at most 30 m, a 65 m search radius, space inside
the next circle, a directly walkable launch point, clear overhead space, clear
horizontal traversal rays and a conservative jet-energy estimate plus reserve.
Only three shortlisted roofs and two enemy-exposure rays per roof are checked.

The original local navigator walks to the launch point without boosting. After
slowing down, the controller jumps and jets vertically until feet clear the roof,
then traverses slowly. When the footprint is supported, jets stop so gravity can
land the mech. On the roof it moves toward the refuge point and holds still.
Outgoing fire is suppressed during refuge to avoid resetting regeneration.
The original aiming, upgrade and pickup logic remains intact. Zone urgency,
unsafe future circle, an enemy on the roof, low energy during approach/climb,
failed approach timeout or falling below the roof cancel refuge. Retry is delayed
five seconds. Original functions and policy remain the fallback.

No EXP-003 retaliation, EXP-005 kiting, EXP-006 combination or EXP-007 boost latch
is included. This is one isolated roof-refuge behavior against the 2.25 baseline.

## Limits and evaluation

These fixtures do not simulate authoritative mech physics. Energy and clearance
estimates omit some acceleration, push/slide and partial-footprint effects.
Traversals sample center/height rays, not a full swept cylinder. Tall/wide roofs,
indirect approaches and bridges are excluded. Distant shooters can still see an
exposed roof, and house bots may climb. The controller may waste time or energy,
fall, or delay useful combat; only the official score can decide its value.
